Questions

How long does the search take?

Four months from the day you’re charged. Outreach starts in your third week. Seeing good options when it ends? Renew to keep searching.

Is a real person involved?

Yes. Sophia’s curator team reviews your interview, shapes your brief with you and meets you to calibrate. I do the research and outreach, and your curator reviews every woman before she reaches you.

When will I meet someone?

As soon as a woman says yes and passes my interview. I won’t promise a date, but I will promise the work: 1,000 or more women approached on your behalf.

What does it cost?

$9,990 for four months, once. No subscription. Or pay over time with Affirm, as low as $323/mo.*

Will the women know who I am?

Not at first. I describe you without your name, and share your profile only with women I’ve interviewed.

How do you find and reach them?

I identify 1,000+ women who fit your brief, wherever they are, then reach them through targeted ads, direct outreach and personal notes. Your name stays private during outreach.

Can I pause the search?

Yes, once, for up to four weeks after outreach begins, say if you start seeing someone I’ve presented. Your clock stops, and the search resumes when the pause ends, or sooner if you ask, so the women I’ve found don’t go cold.

What is the Sophia Guarantee?

If my search returns no one you want to meet, I refund your fee in full. It’s decided when the search ends.

Why does Sophia offer a money-back guarantee?

I’m not aware of another matchmaker that offers one. I want my incentives to match yours, so I’m betting on my own work.

How does Sophia decide who to accept?

Sophia’s membership team accepts you when they believe I can succeed for you. With the guarantee, I’m betting on it. If your search looks harder than four months allows, I may ask for a consultation to revise the scope: more time and budget, or relaxing a few filters once you’ve seen the tradeoffs.

How far does the search go?

As far as it takes. For one client, a founder who trains like an athlete, I considered more than 1,700 women against his brief.

Can I pay over time?

Yes. As low as $323/mo with Affirm, subject to approval.* You’ll see your options at checkout.
